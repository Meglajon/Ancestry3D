Layout
Full circle surrounds you. Half fan puts everyone in front of you. Tree lays the generations out in rows, like a family tree chart.

A Battle in Time. A crooked heir hunter is rubbing your ancestors out of the registers, one generation at a time. You have a battered estate car that runs on parish ink, and every year your family ever lived through to drive it in.

Top-down driving, missions for cash, tickets to the countries your people came from, and a time dial for the years they lived in. Your progress is saved on this device.

Visualisations

GalaxyEvery person is a star coloured by when they were born; you are the sun in the middle. Drag to spin it, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap a star to read about them. Fly-through flies out from you along your longest line. Growing treeYou are the trunk. Watch the branches grow back in time a generation at a time, or play history forward until every line reaches you. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap someone to read about them. VideoRecord video saves the fly-through or the whole growing tree as a video file you can share.
