Visualisations

GalaxyEvery person is a star coloured by when they were born; you are the sun in the middle. Drag to spin it, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap a star to read about them. Fly-through flies out from you along your longest line. Growing treeYou are the trunk. Watch the branches grow back in time a generation at a time, or play history forward until every line reaches you. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap someone to read about them. VideoRecord video saves the fly-through or the whole growing tree as a video file you can share.
